TONY'S DIARY
The Missing Pages: DEC 2021 - MAY 2022

ON COVID ORIGINS - "A CRITICAL FACT THAT SHOULD BE DEVELOPED IN THE BOOK"
Dr. Fauci was already deciding how to present the COVID-origins controversy in his book. Under «****Very important
issue to discuss in the book," he wrote that "the entire controversy with China as the source of the outbreak and

the lab leak theory stems from the tension between Trump and China." He blamed President Trump's cancellation
of EcoHealth Alliance's Wuhan grant for the "deomization of Peter Dascak" and "an emergence of the theory of a
lab leak," reminding himself to "emphasize in the book" that it "really stemmed from Trump" and calling that "a
critical fact that should be developed in the book" (March 21, 2022). He also flagged a Time article he described as
explaining "why it is not important and it may take years to determine" the origin- "We need to incorporate this in
the book" (February 24, 2022).

ON DEFUSE - "COULD LIKELY REQUIRE PSCO OVERSIGHT"
The night before his Senate testimony, and hours after Project Veritas released a Marine Corps major's memo to the

Department of Defense Inspector General concerning Eco Health Alliance's rejected DEFUSE proposal to DARPA,
Dr. Fauci dismissed the release as a "smear campaign" and a "nothing burger" but wrote that the proposal "had stuff in

it that could likely require PCO oversight if it had been sent to us," while stressing that NIAID "never even saw the
grant" (January 10, 2022).

ON THE CDC - "THE CDC IS TRULY A DISFUNCTIONAL ORGANIZATION"

Dr. Fauci wrote that "it was I who recommended to the president again through Ron Klain and Jeff that Rochelle
Walensky be made CDC director" (February 17, 2022). After recommending her for the position, he went on to privately
question the science behind CDC and other agencies' policies. During a fight over testing after five days of isolation, he

wrote that Jeff Zients was "adamant" they not say testing shortages were driving the policy: "We have to say that
testing is required at 5 days and let the system work it out whether the tests are available or not" (December 31, 2021).
Days later, Fauci called CDC "truly a dysfunctional organization," but wrote: "I do not want to make this explicit in
writing of the book" (January 2, 2022 [diary entry mislabeled "Jan 2, 2021"]). On the pediatric vaccine, he wrote that
"The data are not solid for A2 dose vaccine" for children 24 months to four years even as "the FDA wants to push to get
approval for the two dose" while waiting for "confirm atory data for a 3 dose regimen" (February 1, 2022).

ON OPTICS - "WE ARE TRYING TO ACT LIKE WE'RE PULLING OUT OF THIS OUTBREAK"
When Ron Klain warned that special mask, vaccine, and testing requirements around President Biden could look like a
"double standard," Dr. Fauci argued "that the special situation with the president warrants that." Biden agreed: "I'm the
fucking President of the United states." Fauci wrote: "I totally agreed with him" (February 16, 2022). Two months later,
Fauci wrote that CDC's 15-minute "close contact" rule was "really based on almost no science" and that Dr. Walensky
"does want to change this guideline which she realizes is not based on any really solid science," but predicted White

House resistance because officials "don't want the president to stop interacting with the public and to be walking

around with a mask all the time since we are trying to act like we're pulling out of this outbreak" (April 13, 2022).

ON TRUMP - "THE MISINFORMATION THAT HE HELPED KINDLE"
Dr. Fauci wrote that Trump was "trying to have it both ways" on vaccines and that "the misinformation that he helped
kindle is one of the reasons why people don't get vaccinate," while saying the Biden administration "does not try to
interfere with or distort the science the way the trump administration did" (January 12, 2022). He later called Title 42
"obviously a pretext" and wrote that former CDC Director Robert Redfield had, "in typical fashion," "caved in to the
Trump administration" (March 13, 2022). After a Trump-appointed federal judge struck down the transportation mask
mandate, Fauci called it "Another egregious example of Trump appointed judges interfering with sound public health
measures" (April 20, 2022).
