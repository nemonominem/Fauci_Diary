The parser inverts within its release; the app re-inverts across all merged sources at load (`rebuildThreadDownLinks`), with source-qualified keys
- **Attachments resolved across sources**: a document shipped in a release is its own `kind:"attachment"` box placed **directly under the email it was attached to** (indented, with an "📎 Attachment of …" back-link), while every attachment only *announced* in the text ("Attached is …", "see attached …", "I am attaching …") becomes an 📎 chip under the message - an **active link** when the words match a released document, otherwise a dimmed tag marked "not in these releases" (never a dead link). Detection, matching and both directions of the link run **app-side at load** (`inferAttachments`, `orderAttachmentsAfterParents`) on cleaned text across all sources; letterheads, disclaimers and plain verbs are filtered out. Found 31 attachment mentions across the three releases, 1 resolved (Kurilla's filovirus report), 30 tagged as not released
- **Box-type filter**: every entry has a `kind` (diary / email / attachment, with sms, social, slack, whatsapp, chat, document reserved for the sources still to come). The toolbar's new **Box types** button, next to Jump-to-date, ticks which kinds are listed; the choice persists in `localStorage` and applies to search results, the browse list, the timeline bars and the entry counts, so mixing diaries, emails, attachments, tweets, SMS and Slack gives a true multi-media timeline with no UI change. Fixes: **Done** only closes the panel (it used to re-tick every kind), the cached timeline series is dropped on every filter change (the chart previously kept the unfiltered bars), and a browse window whose entries are all filtered out widens - or jumps to the nearest listed box - instead of rendering an empty "No entries found" panel
